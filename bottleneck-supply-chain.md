# The Bottleneck Supply Chain

Reference for the `bottleneck` research category: which suppliers sell into
the constraint.

## The logic

If the binding constraint on AI buildout is power and permits rather than
chips, then the companies selling the solution to *that* constraint have
pricing power, and the ones selling chips are gated by it.

This is a structural observation, not a forecast. It is testable: lead times
and order backlogs are reported quantities, and they move before revenue does.

## Layers, ordered by how binding each is

### 1. Generation equipment — the hardest constraint

Gas turbine capacity is sold out years forward across the major
manufacturers. This is the single tightest link in the chain, and the reason
six of seventeen initial Texas Energy Fund projects withdrew was partly
equipment procurement constraints.

- **GE Vernova** — 7HA.03 H-class turbines are specified in the CPV Basin
  Ranch project. Named in Texas filings.
- **Siemens Energy**
- **Mitsubishi Power**
- Reciprocating engine (RICE) peakers for faster deployment — LCRA's
  Timmerman unit is one.

Track: order backlog, reported lead times, slot availability, price per kW.

### 2. Electrical infrastructure — transformers and switchgear

Large power transformers have multi-year lead times independent of turbines.
A project can have land, financing, a turbine slot and still wait on a
transformer.

- **Eaton**, **Schneider Electric**, **ABB**, **Hitachi Energy**,
  **Vertiv** (power and thermal management for data centers specifically)

Track: backlog, book-to-bill, announced capacity expansions.

### 3. Engineering and construction

- **Quanta Services**, **MYR Group**, **Primoris Services** — transmission
  and substation construction
- EPC capacity is itself constrained by skilled labour

Track: backlog, awarded contracts in ERCOT territory.

### 4. Gas supply and midstream

Behind-the-meter gas generation needs delivered fuel. **Energy Transfer** has
reported agreements exceeding 1 Bcf/d tied to this demand.

Track: announced supply agreements, pipeline capacity additions in the
Permian and North Texas.

### 5. Independent power producers

The ones actually building and owning the generation. Several are TxEF
recipients, so awards and withdrawals are directly observable.

- **NRG Energy** — largest TxEF participant. Three tracked projects.
  Had an advantage because turbines were already on site.
- **Constellation Energy**
- **Vistra** — major ERCOT generator
- **LCRA**, **Rayburn Electric Cooperative** — public/cooperative entities,
  not investable, but their awards signal program direction

### 6. Operators with existing capacity

The counterintuitive winners. If nobody new can energize, whoever is already
running has scarcity pricing. Digital Realty posted a company-record 25.4%
cash rent increase on renewals in Q2 2026 while US vacancy sat at 1.4%.

- **Digital Realty**, **Equinix** — operating capacity, pricing power
- **CoreWeave** and neoclouds — credit-stressed, so the opposite exposure

### 7. Water

An independent gate that can kill an otherwise complete project. Cooling
technology and water treatment suppliers sit here, as do groundwater
conservation district decisions.

## How to write a falsifiable claim in this category

The temptation is to write "X benefits from the turbine shortage." That is
unfalsifiable and the ledger will reject it.

Falsifiable versions:

- "GE Vernova reports gas turbine backlog above $X in its Q4 2026 results"
- "At least two additional TxEF In-ERCOT loan recipients withdraw before
  2027-06-30"
- "Digital Realty reports renewal rent increases above 15% cash basis in
  Q4 2026"
- "Energy Transfer announces at least one new data center supply agreement
  above 0.5 Bcf/d before 2027-03-31"

Each names an entity, a number and a date, and each resolves against a
published document.

## The honest limit on edge

Coverage density varies and it matters.

Mega caps are saturated. Every institutional desk has an analyst on GE
Vernova and Eaton, and public filings are priced within minutes. You will not
beat them on speed.

Smaller suppliers and the Texas regulatory layer are less covered. A PUCT
docket entry or a TxEF withdrawal is genuinely under-watched relative to an
8-K from a megacap. If any edge exists it lives there, and it is an edge in
*noticing earlier than general coverage*, not earlier than the market.

Do not assume a correct structural read converts to a tradeable move. The
conversion from "this company's backlog will grow" to "this stock rises" runs
through valuation, positioning and sentiment, none of which this system
observes. That gap is why the ledger scores claims about world events rather
than about prices.
